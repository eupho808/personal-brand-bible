# Case Study Template

> Use this structure for every portfolio case study.

A case study is not a longer README. It is a story about how a project came to be, why decisions were made, and what was learned.

---

## 1. Overview

One paragraph summarizing the project, its purpose, and its current status.

Example:  
*AI English Journey is a learning operating system that guides one learner from beginner English to exam readiness. It combines structured content, adaptive algorithms, and AI feedback. The project is currently in development, with the foundation architecture complete.*

---

## 2. Problem

Describe the real problem. Make it specific and personal when possible.

- What was wrong with existing solutions?
- Why did it matter to you?
- What would happen if the problem stayed unsolved?

---

## 3. Research / Context

What did you explore before building?

- Existing tools and their limitations.
- What users (including you) actually needed.
- Any constraints: budget, time, technology.

Keep this section short. Do not turn it into a literature review.

---

## 4. Constraints

List the constraints that shaped the project.

- Limited budget.
- No access to paid tutors.
- Must work locally.
- Must be maintainable by one person.
- Must fit within the ecosystem design system.

---

## 5. Architecture

Describe the high-level architecture.

- Stack.
- Key components.
- How data flows.
- Why these choices were made.

Include a diagram if it helps.

---

## 6. Development

Describe how the project was built.

- What was built first and why.
- Key milestones.
- Tools and AI assistance used.
- Major iterations.

Focus on decisions, not just a list of features.

---

## 7. Tradeoffs

Be honest about tradeoffs.

- What did you choose not to do?
- What was simplified?
- What risks remain?
- What would you do differently with more resources?

---

## 8. Lessons

What did you learn?

- Technical insights.
- Product insights.
- Personal insights.

Use the first person. Be specific.

---

## 9. Future

What comes next?

- Short-term goals.
- Open questions.
- Possible expansions.

---

## 10. Links

- GitHub repository
- Live demo if available
- Related documentation
- Related blog posts

---

## Length

Target: 800–1500 words.

A case study should be long enough to tell the story, but short enough to hold attention.

---

## Tone

- Personal but professional.
- Honest about limitations.
- No hype.
- No claims that cannot be supported.
